To start, say "Fall in." Your soldiers begin scattered across the field; calling FALL IN forms them up at attention, but ragged. Next say "Cover" (left arms up, everyone lines up behind the man in front), then "Recover" (arms down, aligned). At the halt, "At close interval… MARCH!" puts right hands on hips with elbows touching the next man and closes the files toward the right file; "Recover" drops the hands and the formation stays at close interval until "Normal interval… MARCH!" opens it back up (that only changes the spacing; it doesn't straighten a ragged formation). "Cover" at close interval also restores normal interval and lines everyone up. Then say "Forward… MARCH!" to step off. At the halt, "Fall out" breaks them up to wander again (score kept); "Fall in" forms them back up. A wrong call for the moment flashes a ! over the drill sergeant for a second; nothing else happens.
Command your formation by voice. Give the preparatory command ("Column right…"), then emphasize the command of execution ("MARCH!"). Make it louder, change your pitch on it, or pause briefly before it.
Recruit: any one of those counts. Soldier: any two. Drill Sergeant: all of them, plus a short, sharp execution called on the correct foot. Commands only: no voice check, for warm-up.
Quick list: Fall, IN (start here) · Fall, OUT · COVER · RECOVER · At close interval, MARCH · Normal interval, MARCH · Forward, MARCH · Platoon, HALT · Column right/left, MARCH · Column half right/left, MARCH · Right/Left flank, MARCH · Rear, MARCH · Counter column, MARCH · Mark time, MARCH · Half step, MARCH · Quick/Double time, MARCH · Change step, MARCH · Right/Left step, MARCH · (1–4) steps to the Right/Left, MARCH · Backward, MARCH · Right/Left/About, FACE · Parade, REST · Stand at, EASE · AT EASE · Platoon, ATTENTION · Dress right, DRESS · Eyes, RIGHT · Ready, FRONT · Open/Close ranks, MARCH · Extend/Close, MARCH.

Snake: soldiers start scattered; say "Fall in" to form up (bumping walls before that doesn't count), then "Cover" and "Recover" to straighten the ragged ranks. Then pick up recruits (gold) to grow the formation. Marching into a wall or into your own formation = FORMATION BROKE.

Command Reference

Based on TC 3-21.5, Drill and Ceremonies. Foot is the foot that should be striking the ground when you call the command of execution. Squad, Platoon, and Flight are all accepted as the unit word.
Voiced cadences
Tap ♪ Cadences, pick one (or 🔀 Shuffle) and tap On. While the platoon marches, the DS calls each line and the platoon sounds off, every line on the left foot, at quick or double time. Keep calling commands: the cadence drops in volume while you talk, ignores its own lyrics, and picks up where it left off on a left foot after your command executes (or when you march again after a halt).
Getting heard on the drill pad
• Hold the phone 6 to 12 inches from your mouth, and don't cover the mic.
• Call the preparatory command crisply, pause slightly, then punch the command of execution.
• Wind and traffic noise drown out speech: turn your back to the wind or cup the phone.
• Clipped drill calls ("colm lef, HARCH", "fowahd, HUT", "platoon, HALT") are fine on Word clarity: Lenient.
• If Safari keeps hearing one command as something odd, use 🎙 Train my voice on the start screen.
• Talk that isn't a drill command is ignored. The status line shows Heard "…" → understood as … for each accepted command.
